SALVAGE BUILDER

Every number in the Salvage Shop: what a scrap pays, what an upgrade step costs, and what each stat's step gives. This makes one file, com.sugarheady.gamemodeforge.salvageshop.rules.json, for your BepInEx\config folder (beside the .cfg files). Salvage Shop writes the defaults there the first time it runs, so there is always one to replace. No restart needed: the file is re-read when it changes — the next gun built and the next time the SALVAGE screen opens use it. An upgrade is stored as a count of steps, so changing a stat's amount changes what every upgrade already bought is worth.

Presets

The shipped rules. A scrap pays 2 Ex and a 50% chance of 1 Bond; scrapping an upgraded item refunds 100% of what it cost. A step costs n Ex at the item's step n, and Bond joins after step 5 (6-9: 1, 10-14: 2, 15: 3…).
Faster upgrades: 3 Ex and a 75% chance of Bond a scrap, and Bond only from step 9.
Slower: 1 Ex and a 25% chance of Bond a scrap, a half refund, and every step 2 Ex dearer than the one before.
For trying it out: 10 Ex and 2 Bond from every scrap, so two or three scraps buy a handful of steps on anything.

Scrap — what a weapon or gadget pays

Every scrap pays the Ex, and the Bond on the chance. Scrapping is from the vault only, and only weapons and gadgets. An item with salvage upgrades also hands back the refund % of the Ex and Bond spent on it (rounded down), on top of its scrap. Ex and Bond are two ingredients the game ships and never uses, so a save holding them loads with the mod off and nothing else hands them out. The other five are the shop's own currencies — scrap paid in one of those also feeds the shop. Face is left for the game and is not offered.

The ladder — what a step costs

The ladder runs per item: each module counts its own steps, whichever stats they went into, and a step is priced at that item's next step number. Ex is Ex at step 1 + added × (step − 1). Bond is 0 up to and including the "from after" step; after it, step ÷ every rounded down (never less than 1), times the amount. No cap. Any stat below can carry its own ladder instead.

Weapon and gadget stats

A step of one stat adds up, of the gun's own number: three +10% damage steps are +30% of its hit. Only the stats that mean something on a gun are offered for it: no explosion or spark on a beam or on the two gadgets that fire a unit (Field Generator, Teardrop); range is the lifetime on a lobbed or rocket shot; speed raises a rocket's thrust too; a gadget's fire rate is its cooldown and its reduce cost its activation cost. Reduce cost also cuts the per-shot cost of every add-on module on that gun. On a gadget, ten steps stop at an activation cost of 0.001, not 0: free in practice, and a gadget whose resource the ship has no tank for still cannot fire, as in the stock game.

Levels

+1 level on a shield, the Power Core, the augmentations that scale with level, and the drone gadgets (a drone gadget's level is its drone count, so +1 level is +1 drone). The card shows it as the game's own "+N" badge.

Ship upgrades — capacity and regen

Any ship upgrade (the UP, REGEN and SHIELD modules) can take the capacity or the regen a second of any of the seven resources. The defaults are the game's own step a level; Fuel and Electron regen have no module in the game. Regen waits the resource's own delay after it last went down (Health 2 s, Stamina 0.5 s, the rest 1.5 s).

The added explosion and spark

An added explosion or spark goes off on hit and when a timed shot expires, the same as the game's own add-ons; with the box above, a plain shot's explosion also goes off at its range (the game has no spark-at-range switch). It costs nothing a shot by default — it was bought with scrap. A shot that cannot pay simply fires without it, the way a game add-on does when its tank runs dry. The explosion is in the gun's own damage type.

In the game

    
    
      
  • At a station, the grid tab's right panel has a third sub-tab, SALVAGE, beside
      SHOP and VAULT. It has two modes, SALVAGE and UPGRADE, and one slot. Drag a
      module into the slot (anywhere on the panel works); the vault stays on screen under it.
    • 
      
  • Pad: RT steps VAULT → SHOP → SALVAGE (LT back), and on SALVAGE switches the
      mode. A sends the highlighted module to the slot, the stick picks a stat, Y
      confirms, B takes it out, X ticks quick salvage.
    • 
      
  • Mouse and keys: click a stat to pick it and again to buy it (or R);
      right-click the slot (or X) to take it out; G switches the mode, C ticks
      quick salvage.
    • 
      
  • QUICK SALVAGE skips the "salvage it?" ask — never for an upgraded item or a
      ranked gun (Weapon Forge), which always ask. It lives in
      com.sugarheady.gamemodeforge.salvageshop.cfg.
    • 
      
  • The GAME MODE FORGE tab in Options has the on/off, Scrap refund of upgrades,
      Bond chance a scrap, Ex a scrap and Quick salvage. The three numbers write
      this same file; everything else here is kept.
    • 
      
  • Upgrades are saved beside the save (salvage.txt) and carry across a New Game
      Plus jump with their items.
