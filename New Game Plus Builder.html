NEW GAME PLUS BUILDER

How much tougher each New Game Plus world gets. This makes one file, com.sugarheady.gamemodeforge.newgameplus.rules.json, for your BepInEx\config folder (beside the .cfg files). New Game Plus writes the defaults there the first time it runs, so there is always one to replace. No restart needed: the rules are read when a world begins — at each jump, and at the start of a run for the first world — and a world keeps the rules it began with, through every save and Continue.

Presets

The shipped rules. The first world is the game as it comes; from NEW GAME PLUS 1, enemies have +50% health a plus on a share that starts at 25% and rises 25% a plus, every room spends +30% a plus more on enemies, and every enemy — and the world's hazards — does +100% damage a plus. The enemy weapon stats, the station cost, money drops, harder enemies earlier, your enemy table and the first-world switch are off.
For trying it out: +50% health on every enemy and +30% enemies from the first world (from plus 0), and +100% damage and every enemy weapon stat on every enemy from NEW GAME PLUS 1, and stations cost money there (100, 400, 700…), money drops +25%, both harder-earlier dials at 25%, and your enemy table brings the Raven into the start biome and the Cross Laser anywhere in Flesh — teleport to the Queens with the debug menu to get there.
Hard fast: +100% health a plus on every enemy, +50% enemies a plus, +200% damage a plus, every enemy weapon stat at twice the default, stations at 200, +500 each (the rise growing 200 a world), and both harder-earlier dials at 50% a plus, all from NEW GAME PLUS 1.
No buffs at all — New Game Plus just rerolls the world and keeps your build.

Enemy health

+50% health is the enemy's health ×1.5 — the resource it actually takes damage through (a drone's Caps or Electron, a Crawler's Tech), not its ammo. Coverage is the share of enemies that get it; which ones is fixed per enemy, so the same enemy stays buffed after a Continue. The Queens get it too. Your own drones and minions never do.

Enemy count

Every room's spawn budget, world-wide (so no coverage). More budget means more enemies and sometimes bigger ones. It is a budget, not a head count: a crowded room stops early. It can never add a Queen — the boss rooms get no ordinary enemies.

Enemy damage

+100% damage is double. Every enemy gets it — no coverage — bosses included, and it counts everything they do: shots, beams, blasts, contact hits, electric sparks and the fire they set. It hits everything harder: your ship, your drones, minions and wingmen, a co-op partner. A bullet you knock back keeps its extra damage. With hazards too on, the world's own damage takes the same % on everything it hits, enemies included. Nothing of yours is ever made stronger or weaker, and crates and boxes are nobody's: they hold loot and are left as they are.

Enemy weapons

Each enemy gun stat is a buff of its own, off by default, and each rolls on its own: coverage is the share of enemies that get it (100% = every one), fixed per enemy so a Continue gives the same guns. The Queens get them too; your own drones and minions never do. Damage is not here — Enemy damage above is the one damage control. The minion and mine launchers (the Queen's, the red crosses', the officer's mines) are left alone: what they launch is an enemy with buffs of its own.

Station unlock cost

Each ingredient: amount at the first station you pay for, + each station for every station after it in that world, and + each world for every world past from plus, up to the cap (0 = none). They come out of your vault. Looking at a locked station shows the whole price in your ship's log; the station's own prompt shows the money.
Off by default. Unlocking a station (using it the first time) costs money: the first one you pay for in a world costs first station, each after it more each station more (100, 400, 700…). The ladder starts again in every new world, and the amount it rises by grows by the rise grows a plus each world. The station you start beside is always free, scanner stations are on the same ladder, and the price shows on the station's own unlock prompt. A station you cannot afford stays locked — and gives no fuel — so the safety net (off by default) is there if you want it; a free unlock still counts as a step on the ladder.

Money drops

Off by default. More money pickups, the game's own 10, 20 and 50, from every source you tick: +25% is a quarter more pickups, so a pile of 3 is 3 and a 75% chance of a fourth. No cap unless you set one. Money a weapon or module pays for a kill (Weapon Forge, Module Forge) is that mod's own and is not changed.

BIG COINS past double. Money pickups bump into each other, and hundreds made at one spot froze the game (a Queen at +500% for minutes). So a drop gets at most as many extra pickups as it already had (and never more than 50); the money past that comes as a few big coins — the game's 50, drawn bigger, worth 100 to 100,000. The money is the same either way.

Harder enemies earlier

Two dials, both off by default, each decided when a world is made. Every room has a budget that it spends on enemies, small near the start and bigger further out, and some enemy kinds only appear in rooms past a line ("outer rooms only"). Both stack with Enemy count; a crowded room still stops early. The Queens are never touched.
how far the near rooms' budget moves toward the far rooms': 25% a plus is all the way by the fourth.
how far each "outer rooms only" line moves toward the start: 25% a plus and the lines are gone by the fourth.

The first world

Off by default. On: a new run plays the whole chain one step on — the first world by NEW GAME PLUS 1's rules, NEW GAME PLUS 1 by NEW GAME PLUS 2's, and so on, every buff and the station cost included. The titles still count real worlds (WORLD 1 CLEARED); the arrival line and WORLD INFO say whose rules are in force. It is read when a run starts, and that run keeps it.

How the numbers stack

A buff adds up each plus from the one it starts at: start + added × (plus − from plus), up to the cap. So +50% start and +50% a plus is +50%, +100%, +150%… Nothing before from plus; plus 0 is the first world. Coverage ramps the same way and never passes 100%. Extra projectiles and burst shots are whole numbers instead: add × (1 + (plus − from plus) ÷ every), rounded down inside the brackets, up to the cap.

Health bar colours

    The colours of a buffed enemy's health bar layers. The last
    layer is always the enemy's own bar colour (red for most, and the Queens); the layers above
    it take these colours in order and go round again. A colour too like the one under it is
    skipped. In the game, Layer colours in the GAME MODE FORGE tab steps through the same
    palettes. These are display settings in the .cfg, not part of the rules file
    above, and they apply at once.

In the game instead

    
    
      
  • The GAME MODE FORGE tab in Options has three sliders, Enemy health a
      plus, Enemy count a plus and Enemy damage a plus. Each sets that buff's
      start and its added-a-plus to the same number and writes this same file; everything else
      here is kept. Enemy damage: hazards too is the switch above, in the tab, and so is
      Station unlock cost (on or off; its numbers are here).
    • 
      
  • The same tab has what carries to the next world — ship grid, vault,
      money, shop stock, shop prices, stations count, ingredients ever owned, run time and
      kills (all on), and whether you arrive on full health, fuel and ammo (the default) or
      keep what you had. Those live in
      com.sugarheady.gamemodeforge.newgameplus.cfg, and are read when you jump.
    • 
      
  • Marker on buffed enemies (off by default) puts a small amber ring with a plus
      in it beside each enemy New Game Plus has buffed.
    • 
      
  • Health bars in layers (on by default): a buffed enemy's bar stays its normal
      size and the extra health from Enemy health above is stacked in layers of that
      size — the last layer is the enemy's own bar and colour, the ones above it go green,
      yellow, orange, red, purple, then round again, with an xN beside the bar. So with
      +100% health an enemy has two layers, and with the TEST preset's +50% the top
      layer is half a bar. The Queens' bar too. It is a display setting in the
      .cfg ([Display] HealthBarLayers), not part of this file, and
      applies at once. Layer colours, the row under it, steps through the palettes in
      Health bar colours above; its CUSTOM is your own list from the cfg.

Your enemy table

    
    Every enemy in the game, and every biome. Put a plus number in a box and from that
    world on the kind may appear in that biome, anywhere in it. A green box is the kind's
    own biome: a number there drops its "outer rooms only" line (a blank one follows the dial
    above). weight is how often it is drawn where it is new (blank = its weight at home);
    cost is what it takes from a room's enemy budget there (blank = its cost at home). The
    first 25 are the ones the biomes place. Below them is every other enemy — the
    Queen, the minions and mines other enemies launch, the ones only special rooms place, your own
    drones, and the ones only the debug menu spawns. They have no home, so their weight is
    0 (never drawn) until you give one, and their cost is 10. ⚠ Every Queen killed
    counts toward the 4, so a Queen from the table can end a world sooner. Off and empty by
    default. The table scrolls in its own box — sideways for the biomes, down for the enemies — with the names and the biome headings held in place.
